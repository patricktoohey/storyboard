# Style bible

## Look
- Format: 2.39:1 anamorphic, 35mm film grain, muted teal/amber palette
- Light: storm daylight, overcast and hard-edged; the lamp is the only warm source
- Lenses: 40mm anamorphic for wides, 75mm for close-ups

## Characters
- **Maren**: keeper, late 50s, weathered face, grey braid, oversized yellow oilskin coat
  (ref: bible/refs/maren.webp)

## Locations
- **Island**: black basalt rock, white-and-red striped lighthouse, constant spray
